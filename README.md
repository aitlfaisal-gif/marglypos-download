# MarglyPOS for Windows

Installer for MarglyPOS, the point-of-sale and inventory system, to run on your own PC.

**[Download MarglyPOS-Setup.exe](https://github.com/aitlfaisal-gif/marglypos-download/releases/latest/download/MarglyPOS-Setup.exe)** (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

1. Open `MarglyPOS-Setup.exe`. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
2. Enter your shop name, owner, admin email, phone and password.
3. Double-click the **MarglyPOS** icon on the desktop. The POS opens at http://localhost:8090.
4. To open the shop from your phone or another computer, double-click **MarglyPOS Online**.

Your data stays on your PC (`C:\MarglyPOS\data`) and is backed up every day to `Documents\MarglyPOS Backup`.
To update, run a newer installer; your shop and data are kept.
